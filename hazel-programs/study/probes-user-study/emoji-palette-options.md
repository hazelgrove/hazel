## Rationale

The mushroom 🍄 really sells the "forest floor at night" vibe.

The lotus 🪷 and cherry blossom 🌸 feel ethereal.

The fern 🌿 and clover ☘️ are grounded but still magical.

Avoiding conventional vegetables (🥕 🌽 🍅) keeps it feeling mystical rather than practical farming.
